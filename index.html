Find free books on Open Library and send them to your Xteink.

    Start with a title or an author

    Free downloads are mostly older, public-domain books.

    Library

    Books you've downloaded to your phone. Mark them as sent once they're on the reader.

    Send to Xteink

    1. Open Hotspot Mode on the reader.

      The screen shows a network called CrossPoint-Reader.

    2. Join that Wi-Fi network on your phone.

      Settings → Wi-Fi → CrossPoint-Reader. Your phone will say there's no internet; that's expected.

    3. Open the reader's upload page.

      Tap the button below, or scan the second QR code on the reader.

      Open upload page

      If the page doesn't load, try http://crosspoint.local/

    4. Choose the books from your folder.

      Pick them from the folder Safari saves downloads to, then upload.

    5. Come back and mark them as sent.

      Rejoin your normal Wi-Fi when you're done.

    Keep your books in one folder

    On iPhone, go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Downloads and choose a folder such as “Xteink” in Files. Every book you download here will land there.
